Easy bulgogi

Ingredients

  • 450g skirt steak, tenderloin or top sirloin, slice into ⅛ inch-thick slices
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 green onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 3 tablespoons water
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame seeds, coarsely ground
  • ½ teaspoon ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Make marinade by combining the garlic, green onion, soy sauce, sugar, water, sesame oil, sesame seeds, and ground black pepper in a large bowl. Mix it well with a spoon until the sugar is well dissolved.
  2. Add the sliced beef and mix everything by hand until the beef is covered in the marinade. Keep in the fridge at least 30 minutes.
  3. Cook on a pan or a grill, and transfer to a plate or a cast iron plate to serve with rice, lettuce, ssamjang, and more side dishes.
  4. To eat, wrap a spoonful of rice, a piece of bulgogi, and a little bit of ssamjang in a lettuce leaf. Fold it over and eat. Repeat!

Bulgogi (with pear) (approx 325 - 350 cals without rice - if serves 4)

Ingredients

  • 450g skirt steak, tenderloin or top sirloin, slice into ⅛ inch-thick slices
  • ½ cup of crushed Korean pear (aka “Asian pear”), or bosc pear
  • ¼ cup onion purée
  • 4 cloves of minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon minced ginger
  • 1 chopped green onion
  • 2 tbs soy sauce
  • 2 tbs brown sugar (or 1 tbs of brown sugar and 1½ tbs rice syrup)
  • a pinch of ground black pepper
  • 1 tbs toasted toasted sesame oil
  • Several thin slices of carrot

Instructions

  1. Mix all the marinade ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Add the sliced beef and mix well.
  3. You can grill, pan-fry, or BBQ right after marinating, but it’s best to keep it in the fridge and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes, or overnight for a tougher cut of beef.
  4. Grill over medium-high heat. Best is over an open flame on a grill or cast iron grill pan or skillet, but stir-frying will also work, if that’s all you have.
  5. Serve with Korean side dishes (banchan) like kimchi as well as lettuce, ssamjang, and garlic cloves to make lettuce wraps, like I do in the video. Just put some meat on a lettuce leaf with some ssamjang and a clove of garlic, wrap it up and eat it in one bite! Everyone makes their own wrap, one by one, until they are finished.

  1. Soak the dried shiitake mushrooms in warm water for several hours until they’re soft.
  2. Squeeze out any excess water and slice each mushroom thinly.
  3. Slice some white mushrooms, carrot, and onion.
  4. Mix all of it together in the marinade.

Braised chicken legs (kikkoman) (approx 650 calories)

https://kikkomanusa.com/homecooks/recipes/braised-chicken-legs/

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken legs
  • 1 small onion, slivered
  • 2 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
  • 2 teaspoons ginger, minced
  • 1 - 2 jalapenos (or ½ fresno chilli)
  • 1 teaspoon tomato paste
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 teaspoons brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • Bunch of fresh cilantro

Instructions

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 175c. Pat the chicken legs dry and season both sides generously with salt and pepper. ​
  2. Heat a generous amount of neutral oil over medium-high heat in a pan and add in the chicken legs skin side down. Sear skin
  3. side down for 5 minutes until brown, then flip over and sear for another 3 minutes. Remove then set aside.​
  4. In the same pan over medium heat, add onions, garlic, ginger, and chilis and sauté until onions are just translucent (about 1 minute).​
  5. Add in the tomato paste and mix. Continue cooking until slightly brown (about another 1-2 minutes).​
  6. Turn heat up to high, then pour in chicken stock to deglaze, then the coconut milk, soy sauce, lime juice, and brown sugar. Then add in the peppercorns.​
  7. Bring to a boil, then lay in chicken legs skin side up and place in the oven to braise, uncovered, for 45 minutes.​
  8. Remove from the oven, remove chicken legs and set aside, and bring the remaining braising liquid to a boil and reduce by about half.​
  9. Plate your chicken leg, spoon over the sauce, and garnish with a handful of fresh cilantro leaves.

Japanese ramen eggs

(link)

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs, at room temperature (adjust quantity as needed)
  • 3 tablespoons light soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon dark soy sauce (optional, for deeper color)
  • 2 tablespoons mirin (sweet rice wine)
  • 120 ml (½ cup) water

Instructions

  1. Boil the Eggs:
    • Bring a pot of water to a rolling boil. Gently lower the room-temperature eggs into the boiling water using a spoon.
    • Set a timer for 6 minutes for a slightly runny yolk, or adjust the cooking time as desired (7 minutes for a slightly firmer yolk).
  2. Ice Bath:
    • Immediately after boiling, transfer the eggs to a bowl filled with ice water. This stops the cooking process and ensures a soft yolk.
    • Let the eggs chill in the ice bath for at least 15 minutes.
  3. Prepare the Marinade:
    • While the eggs chill, combine the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce (if using), mirin, and water in a small saucepan or container.
    • Heat the marinade gently over low heat until just simmering. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.
  4. Marinate the Eggs:
    • Carefully peel the cooled eggs. Gently crack the shells all over to help the marinade penetrate.
    • Place the peeled eggs in a jar or container with a tight-fitting lid. Pour the cooled marinade over the eggs, ensuring they are completely submerged.
  5. Refrigerate:
    • Refrigerate the marinated eggs for at least 12 hours, ideally longer for a deeper flavor. The longer they marinate, the richer the flavor becomes, and the yolk will develop a jammier consistency.
    • The eggs will keep well in the refrigerator for up to 3-5 days.
  6. Serve:
    • Once ready to serve, remove a marinated egg from the jar and slice it in half with a sharp knife.
    • The contrast of the browned whites and vibrant yellow yolk is a delightful sight. Enjoy your ramen eggs on top of a steaming bowl of ramen, or savor them on their own with some rice.

Tips

  • Use room temperature eggs for even cooking; cold eggs take longer to cook and can result in uneven yolks.
  • Adjust the marinating time to your preference; for a milder flavor, marinate for a shorter time.
  • Experiment with different flavorings in the marinade, such as sake, ginger, or garlic.
  • Be gentle when peeling the eggs after boiling to avoid tearing the whites.

(Stella parks) Glossy Fudge Brownie Recipe

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces unsalted butter (about 3 sticks; 340g)
  • 6 ounces finely chopped dark chocolate, about 72% (about 1 cup; 170g)
  • 16 ounces granulated sugar (about 2 1/4 cups; 450g)
  • 2 ounces light brown sugar (about 1/4 cup, packed; 55g)
  • 1 3/4 tsp (4g) Diamond Crystal kosher salt (for table salt, use about half by volume or the same weight)
  • 6 large eggs, straight from the fridge (about 10 1/2 ounces; 295g)
  • 1/2 ounce vanilla extract (about 1 tbsp; 15g)
  • 1 tsp instant espresso powder, optional
  • 4 1/2 ounces all-purpose flour (about 1 cup, spooned; 125g)
  • 4 ounces Dutch-process cocoa powder (about 1 1/3 cups, spooned; 115g)

Directions:

  1. Prep: Adjust oven rack to the middle position and preheat to 350°F (180°C). Line a 9x13-inch anodized aluminum baking pan with foil, covering the bottom and long sides. Grease lightly with pan spray, if desired.
  2. Brown Butter: In a 3-quart stainless steel saucier, melt the butter over medium-low heat. Once melted, increase heat to medium and simmer, stirring until the butter is golden yellow and silent. Remove from heat and stir in dark chocolate until melted.
  3. Whip Ingredients: In a stand mixer fitted with a whisk attachment, combine granulated sugar, brown sugar, salt, eggs, vanilla, and instant espresso powder (if using). Whip on medium-high until thick and fluffy, about 8 minutes.
  4. Mix Batter: Sift together flour and cocoa powder. Reduce mixer speed to low and slowly pour in the warm chocolate-butter mixture. Add the flour-cocoa mixture all at once, and mix until roughly combined. Finish by folding the batter with a spatula to ensure it’s evenly mixed.
  5. Bake: Pour the batter into the prepared pan. Bake until the brownies are glossy and just barely firm, about 30 minutes, or until they reach an internal temperature of 205°F (96°C).
  6. Cool and Slice: Cool the brownies to room temperature. Use a butter knife to loosen the sides, then lift the brownies out by the foil. Cut into 24 squares and store in an airtight container with wax paper between layers.

(Claire saffitz) Malted “Forever” Brownies

Ingredients

  • Butter for the pan
  • ¼ cup Dutch-process cocoa powder (0.7 oz / 20g)
  • 5 ounces semisweet chocolate, 64-68% cacao, coarsely chopped (142g)
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, cut into pieces (3 oz / 85g)
  • ¼ cup neutral oil, such as vegetable or grapeseed (2 oz / 56g)
  • ½ cup granulated sugar (3.5 oz / 100g)
  • ½ cup packed dark brown sugar (3.5 oz / 100g)
  • 1 large egg (1.8 oz / 50g)
  • 2 large egg yolks (1.1 oz / 32g)
  • 1½ tsp vanilla extract
  • ¾ cup all-purpose flour (3.5 oz / 100g)
  • 2 tbsp malted milk powder (optional, 0.63 oz / 18g)
  • 1 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt (0.11 oz / 3g)
  • 6 ounces milk chocolate, coarsely chopped (170g)

Preparation:

  1. Preheat and Prepare Pan: Arrange an oven rack in the center position and preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Line an 8x8-inch pan with 2 sheets of foil, crossing them over one another and pressing into the corners. Lightly butter the foil and set aside.
  2. Bloom Cocoa: In a large heatproof bowl, whisk the cocoa powder with ¼ cup boiling water (4 oz / 113g) until smooth. This will enhance the flavor of the cocoa.
  3. Melt Chocolate, Butter, and Oil: Add the semisweet chocolate, butter, and oil to the bowl with the cocoa mixture. Set the bowl over a medium saucepan filled with about 1 inch of simmering water (ensuring the bowl doesn't touch the water). Stir occasionally until everything is melted and smooth. Remove the bowl from heat and let it cool to lukewarm.
  4. Add Sugars and Eggs: Whisk in the granulated sugar and brown sugar into the chocolate mixture (it may look grainy or separated, which is normal). Add the egg, egg yolks, and vanilla, whisking vigorously until the mixture is thick, smooth, and glossy.
  5. Mix Dry Ingredients: Add the flour, malted milk powder (if using), and salt to the chocolate mixture. Whisk slowly to combine, then whisk vigorously for 45 seconds until the batter becomes very thick.
  6. Fold in Chocolate and Bake: Fold the chopped milk chocolate into the batter with a spatula. Scrape the batter into the prepared pan, spreading it evenly to the edges.
  7. Bake: Bake the brownies until the surface is shiny and puffed, and the center is dry to the touch but still soft when pressed, 25-30 minutes.
  8. Cool, Chill, and Cut: Let the brownies cool in the pan for about 1 hour. Refrigerate for another hour until the pan feels cold for a chewier texture. Use the foil to lift the brownies out, transfer to a cutting board, and slice into 16 squares.

Lemon crinkle cookies

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups (313g) all-purpose flour, spooned & leveled
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¾ cup (12 Tbsp; 170g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
  • 1 cup + 2 Tbsp (225g) granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg, at room temperature
  • ¼ cup (60ml) fresh or bottled lemon juice, at room temperature
  • 1 Tbsp packed lemon zest (a lot of zest!)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

For Rolling:

  • 3 Tbsp (35g) granulated sugar (optional)
  • 1 cup (120g) confectioners’ sugar

Instructions

  1. Mix Dry Ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, cornstarch, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
  2. Cream Butter and Sugar: In a large bowl, using a hand mixer or stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment, beat the butter and granulated sugar on medium-high speed until light and fluffy, about 1 minute. Add the egg, lemon juice, lemon zest, and vanilla extract. Beat on high speed for 1 minute, until fully combined. The mixture may look curdled—this is normal.
  3. Incorporate Dry Ingredients: Gradually add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients, beating on low speed until fully combined. The dough will be thick, creamy, and sticky.
  4. Chill the Dough: Cover the dough tightly and chill in the refrigerator for at least 3 hours (or up to 3 days).
  5. Prepare for Baking: Remove the dough from the fridge. If chilled for more than 3 hours, let the dough sit at room temperature for about 10 minutes for easier handling. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C) and line baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone baking mats.
  6. Roll the Dough: Scoop and roll dough into 1-tablespoon balls (about 20g each). To prevent the confectioners' sugar from melting during baking, first roll each ball in granulated sugar, then generously coat in confectioners’ sugar. For a heavily sugared appearance, roll the dough 2-3 times in confectioners’ sugar. Place cookies 3 inches apart on the prepared baking sheets.
  7. Bake: Bake for 12-13 minutes, or until the edges are set and the centers are still soft. If the cookies haven’t spread by minute 9, remove them from the oven and bang the baking sheet on the counter 2-3 times to encourage spreading. Return to the oven and finish baking.
  8. Cool: Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely. The cookies will slightly deflate as they cool.
