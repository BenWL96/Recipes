Garlic Mushroom Wafu Pasta

Lunch, Korean & Japanese
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • approximately 710 calories (serving 2)
  • 2 servings spaghetti
  • 1 pack pancetta, diced
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 pack beech mushrooms, sliced
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tsp shoyu (Japanese soy sauce)
  • 2 tsp fish sauce
  • 4 tsp oyster sauce
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • Scallions, chopped (for garnish)
  • Shredded nori (for garnish)
  • Pasta water (as needed)

Method

  1. Fry the Pancetta: In a skillet, fry the diced pancetta over medium heat until crispy. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate and set aside.
  2. Cook the Noodles: In a pot, boil water and add the spaghetti. Cook for a couple of minutes shorter than the typical cook time as the pasta will finish cooking in the sauce.
  3. Sauté Garlic and Mushrooms: In the same skillet used for the pancetta, melt 3 tbsp of butter over medium-low heat. Add the minced garlic and sliced mushrooms. Cook until softened, about 3-4 minutes. Stir in the oyster sauce, fish sauce, and shoyu.
  4. Combine Pasta and Sauce: Once the noodles are done, drain them and add them directly to the mushroom and garlic mixture. Increase the heat and mix well.
  5. Emulsify the Sauce: Gradually add pasta water while mixing until the sauce emulsifies, using more water than you think for the best consistency.
  6. Finish the Dish: When the noodles are al dente, remove from heat. Add the grated Parmesan, crispy pancetta, and chopped scallions. Toss until the cheese melts into the sauce.
  7. Serve: Plate the pasta immediately, garnished with shredded nori. Enjoy your delicious wafu pasta!
