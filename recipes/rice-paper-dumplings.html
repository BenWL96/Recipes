Rice paper dumplings

Lunch & Dinner, Chinese
Approx 500 cals if serves 4Original recipe · okonomikitchen.com ↗
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

Rice paper dumplings skirt

  • Dumpling filling
  • Rice paper
  • ¼ cup water
  • 1tsp all purpose flour
  • 1 tsp cornstarch or potato starch

Meat filling (makes approx 12 - 14 dumplings)

  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp ginger, grated
  • 2 green onions
  • ½ medium onion
  • 80g finely chopped cabbage or bokchoi
  • ½ medium carrot
  • 40g mushrooms
  • 452g minced meat
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp white pepper or ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil

Dipping sauce

  • 2 tbsp chilli oil
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds

Method

  1. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Add ½ medium onion and cook. Then add 2 garlic cloves (minced) and 2 tsp ginger (grated) and cook until fragrant. Add 2 green onions, 80g chopped cabbage, ½ medium carrot and 40g mushrooms - cook until softened (about 3 mins). Add the minced meat, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp black pepper, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sugar, 1tbsp sesame oil, some MSG and cook until brown. Don’t worry if the filling is not completely cooked.
  2. Wrap the filling in rice papers (double wrapped).
  3. Prepare skirt mix by mixing ¼ cup of water, 1 tsp plain flour, 1sp cornstarch
  4. Cook dumplings:
    1. Pan:
      1. Heat non-stick pan with oil. Brush tops of dumplings with excess oil. Cook dumplings until golden brown and crispy, and then flip them over.
      2. Pour the slurry in one section of the pan and swirl around the pan to even spread around. Do not let the slurry touch the tops of the dumplings. Do not touch the dumplings and let them cook until the liquid has evaporated and bottoms are golden brown (approx 5 mins).
    2. Airfry:
      1. Preheat air fryer to 200c. Lightly brush the dumplings with oil. Cook for 8 - 13 minutes, flipping halfway through.
      2. Do not cook with the cornstarch skirt!
