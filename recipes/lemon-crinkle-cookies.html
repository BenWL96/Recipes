Lemon crinkle cookies

Desserts, Cookies
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups (313g) all-purpose flour, spooned & leveled
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¾ cup (12 Tbsp; 170g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
  • 1 cup + 2 Tbsp (225g) granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg, at room temperature
  • ¼ cup (60ml) fresh or bottled lemon juice, at room temperature
  • 1 Tbsp packed lemon zest (a lot of zest!)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

For Rolling:

  • 3 Tbsp (35g) granulated sugar (optional)
  • 1 cup (120g) confectioners’ sugar

Method

  1. Mix Dry Ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, cornstarch, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
  2. Cream Butter and Sugar: In a large bowl, using a hand mixer or stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment, beat the butter and granulated sugar on medium-high speed until light and fluffy, about 1 minute. Add the egg, lemon juice, lemon zest, and vanilla extract. Beat on high speed for 1 minute, until fully combined. The mixture may look curdled—this is normal.
  3. Incorporate Dry Ingredients: Gradually add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients, beating on low speed until fully combined. The dough will be thick, creamy, and sticky.
  4. Chill the Dough: Cover the dough tightly and chill in the refrigerator for at least 3 hours (or up to 3 days).
  5. Prepare for Baking: Remove the dough from the fridge. If chilled for more than 3 hours, let the dough sit at room temperature for about 10 minutes for easier handling. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C) and line baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone baking mats.
  6. Roll the Dough: Scoop and roll dough into 1-tablespoon balls (about 20g each). To prevent the confectioners' sugar from melting during baking, first roll each ball in granulated sugar, then generously coat in confectioners’ sugar. For a heavily sugared appearance, roll the dough 2-3 times in confectioners’ sugar. Place cookies 3 inches apart on the prepared baking sheets.
  7. Bake: Bake for 12-13 minutes, or until the edges are set and the centers are still soft. If the cookies haven’t spread by minute 9, remove them from the oven and bang the baking sheet on the counter 2-3 times to encourage spreading. Return to the oven and finish baking.
  8. Cool: Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely. The cookies will slightly deflate as they cool.
