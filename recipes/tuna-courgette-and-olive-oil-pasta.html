Tuna courgette and olive oil pasta

Lunch & Dinner, Pasta & Grains
Original recipe · instagram.com ↗
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • 2 - 3 courgettes
  • 1 - 2 lemons
  • 2 - 3 garlic cloves
  • Olives
  • Parmesan
  • Basil
  • Pasta
  • tuna

Method

  1. Fry courgette in olive oil and butter
  2. Cook pasta
  3. Peel skin off lemon, slice into strips
  4. Add lemon and garlic to courgette and cook
  5. Add pasta to courgettes
  6. Add parmesan, basil, olives and tuna
