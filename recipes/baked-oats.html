Baked oats

Breakfast, Breakfast
Approx 410 - 425 if serves 4
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas (or 180g apple sauce)
  • 200g oats
  • 60g protein powder
  • 420ml milk
  • 1tsp baking powder
  • 140g raspberries
  • spread

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180c
  2. Mash banana, add oats, protein powder, baking powder and milk. Stir
  3. Add raspberries and dollop yogurt/spread
  4. Bake for 35 mins

If divided by 3 = approx 550 cals

Or : pecan pie baked oats

Orange honey salmon

https://laughingspatula.com/honey-glazed-salmon-with-orange-and-ginger/#recipe

Ingredients

  • 900g salmon
  • ½ cup honey
  • 1 tablespoon grated orange zest (1 orange worth)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh orange juice
  • 1 tablespoon ginger, grated
  • 2 cloves of garlic, grated

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200c.
  2. Line large baking sheet with foil.
  3. Rinse and pat dry the salmon fillet and lay on top of foil.
  4. Liberally salt and pepper entire fillet.
  5. Combine honey, grated ginger, grated garlic, orange zest and orange juice in a small bowl.
  6. Pour over salmon fillet.
  7. Place slices of one orange around fillet.
  8. Bake for about 15-20 minutes or until salmon flakes easily with a fork and reaches 145 degrees. If desired place under broiler for 1-2 minutes to additional carmalize.
