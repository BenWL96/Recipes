Honey yuzu soy sauce chicken

Dinner, Korean & Japanese
Approx 565 - 615 calories if serves 3
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

(link)

  • 6 drumsticks (680g)
  • 5–6 tbsp of yuzu honey marmalade or jam, depending on how sweet you like it
  • 2 tbsp white miso
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp sansho or black pepper
  • 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 tbsp ginger, minced
  • 1/2 tbsp perilla or sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp sriracha, optional
  • green onions, for garnish

Method

  1. Marinate: In a bowl or bag, combine yuzu marmalade, miso, soy sauce, vinegar, salt, pepper, garlic, ginger, oil, and sriracha (if using). Add chicken and coat well. Marinate for at least 30 minutes or up to overnight in the fridge.
  2. Cooking:
    1. Air Fryer: preheat to 375°F (190°C).
      1. First Cook: Place drumsticks in the air fryer basket in a single layer. Cook for 8 minutes.
      2. Glaze + Cook Again: Brush with more marinade, then air fry for another 5 minutes.
      3. Flip + Finish: Flip the chicken and cook for another 8 minutes or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
      4. Char (Optional): Increase temperature to 400°F (200°C) and air fry for 2 minutes, or until slightly charred to your liking.
    2. Oven:
      1. Cook at 190c for 30 - 35 minutes, flipping halfway while brushing on the glaze
  3. Serve: Garnish with sesame seeds and enjoy hot.
