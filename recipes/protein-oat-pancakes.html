Protein oat pancakes

Breakfast, Breakfast
Serves 1 - 30g protein, 430 - 460 cals
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • 60g oats
  • 100g cottage cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • Baking powder

Method

  1. Blend oats, cottage cheese, eggs, small amount of sugar and pinch of salt together
  2. Fry in oil or butter
