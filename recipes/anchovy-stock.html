Anchovy stock

Dinner, Korean & Japanese
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • 7 large dried anchovies, heads and guts removed
  • ⅓ cup of korean radish, or daikon radish
  • 4x5 inch dried kelp
  • 3 green onion roots
  • 4 cups of water
  • 2 cups anchovy stock

Method

  1. Optional: dry roast anchovies in pot over medium heat for few minutes until they smell nutty. Enhances flavour.
  2. Boil for 20 minutes over medium high
  3. Lower hear for another 5 mins
  4. strain
