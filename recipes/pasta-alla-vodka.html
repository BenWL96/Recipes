Pasta alla vodka

Dinner, Pasta & Risotto
650 - 675 if serves 4
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • Kosher salt
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed and peeled
  • 4 oz Parmesan cheese, finely grated
  • 2 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling
  • 1 (4.5-oz) tube double-concentrated tomato paste
  • ½ tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • 2 oz (¼ cup) vodka
  • ¾ cup heavy cream
  • 1 lb rigatoni
  • Fresh basil leaves, for serving

Method

  1. Boil the Water: Fill a large stock pot three-quarters full with water, add a handful of kosher salt, and bring to a boil while you prep the other ingredients.
  2. Prepare Ingredients: Finely chop the onion and smash the garlic cloves. Grate the Parmesan cheese.
  3. Sauté Onions and Garlic: Heat 2 Tbsp of extra-virgin olive oil in a Dutch oven or large pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and smashed garlic. Cook, stirring constantly, until the onions are just beginning to brown around the edges, about 5-7 minutes.
  4. Add Tomato Paste: Stir in the tomato paste and crushed red pepper flakes. Cook, stirring often, until the paste evenly coats the onions and begins to brown, about 5-7 minutes.
  5. Deglaze with Vodka: Pour in the vodka and stir, scraping the bottom of the pot to deglaze it. Reduce heat to low.
  6. Warm Cream: Scoop about ¼ cup of boiling water from the pasta pot and mix it with the heavy cream to gently warm it. Slowly add the warmed cream to the Dutch oven, stirring constantly until a smooth sauce forms. Remove the pot from the heat.
  7. Cook the Pasta: Add the rigatoni to the pot of boiling salted water and cook according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of the pasta water before draining.
  8. Combine Pasta and Sauce: Add the rigatoni to the sauce in the Dutch oven along with ½ cup of the reserved pasta water. Stir to incorporate. Gradually add half of the Parmesan cheese, stirring constantly until melted and the sauce is smooth and glossy.
  9. Adjust Seasoning: Season with salt to taste. If needed, add more reserved pasta water to thin the sauce until it coats the pasta perfectly.
  10. Serve: Divide the pasta among bowls. Top each serving with the remaining Parmesan, drizzle with extra olive oil, and garnish with torn basil leaves.
