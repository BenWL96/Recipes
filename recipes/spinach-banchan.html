Spinach banchan

Lunch & Dinner, Sides & Sauces
Original recipe · instagram.com ↗
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • 1 - 2 bags of spinach
  • 2-3 tsp sesame oil
  • 2-3 tsp soy sauce
  • 2-4 cloves minced garlic
  • Chopped green onions
  • Sesame seeds
  • Gochugaru
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Add spinach to salted boiling water and blanch for 20 seconds, spinach will shrink and turn bright green
  2. Add into an ice bath, then gently wring out the excess water with your hands
  3. Add seasonings and mix
