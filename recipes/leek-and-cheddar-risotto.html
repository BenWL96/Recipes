Leek and cheddar risotto

Lunch & Dinner, Pasta & Grains
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp EVOO
  • 1 leek
  • 300g risotto rice
  • 50ml apple cider vinegar
  • ½ tsp dijon mustard
  • 1L chicken stock
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 125g cheddar
  • Handful chopped fresh chives

Method

  1. Warm butter and EVOO, cook leeks for a few minutes until softened
  2. Add risotto rice and stir for a minute, then add the mustard and apple cider vinegar
  3. Ladle in stock and stir, once absorbed, add another ladle
  4. When rice is al dente, add in peas for one minute and then turn off the heat
  5. Add in the cheese and stir until melted through. Finish with chives and black pepper.
