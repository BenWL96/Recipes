Korean Veggie Pancakes

Lunch & Dinner, Korean
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • For the Pancake Batter:
    • ½ cup all-purpose flour
    • 1 tbsp corn flour
    • ½ cup water
    • 2-2.5 cups of your favorite vegetables, sliced lengthwise (suggestions: spring onions, carrots, cabbage, onions)
  • For Cooking:
    • 1-2 tbsp oil (for frying)
  • For the Dipping Sauce:
    • 2 tbsp soy sauce
    • 1 tbsp vinegar
    • Sesame seeds (to taste)
    • Gochugaru or chili flakes (to taste)

Method

  1. Make the Batter: In a bowl, whisk together the all-purpose flour, corn flour, and water until smooth. Stir in the sliced vegetables until evenly coated.
  2. Heat the Skillet: In a non-stick skillet, heat 1-2 tbsp of oil over medium heat.
  3. Cook the Pancake: Pour the vegetable batter into the skillet, spreading it evenly into a round shape. Gently flatten the top with the back of your spatula.
  4. Cook Evenly: Lower the heat and cook until the pancake is golden brown and cooked through on both sides, flipping halfway. Add more oil if needed.
  5. Prepare the Dipping Sauce: In a small bowl, mix together the soy sauce, vinegar, sesame seeds, and gochugaru or chili flakes.
  6. Serve: Slice the pancake and serve warm with the dipping sauce.
