Salt and pepper chips/smiley faces

Dinner, Sides
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • For the Chips:
    • Frozen thick-cut chips (or fries)
  • For the Seasoning:
    • 10 tsp Chinese 5 spice
    • 10 tsp sugar
    • 5 tsp garlic powder
    • 2.5 tsp white pepper
    • 5 tsp salt
    • 2.5 tsp MSG (optional)
  • For Sautéing:
    • Oil (for frying)
    • 1 onion, chopped
    • 3 cloves garlic, minced
    • 2 bird’s eye chilies, sliced
    • 1 green bell pepper, sliced
    • 1 mild red chili, sliced
    • Spring onions (scallions), chopped (for garnish)
    • Optional: bell pepper

Method

  1. Cook the Chips: Begin by air frying or baking frozen thick-cut chips according to package instructions.
  2. Mix Seasoning: In a bowl, combine the Chinese 5 spice, sugar, garlic powder, white pepper, salt, and MSG (if using) to create the seasoning mix.
  3. Prepare Extra Seasoning (Optional): You can make extra seasoning to keep on hand for future use.
  4. Sauté Aromatics: Heat a wok or frying pan over medium-high heat. Add a splash of oil and sauté the chopped onion, minced garlic, and sliced bird’s eye chilies until fragrant.
  5. Prep for Quick Cooking: This cooking process is quick, so ensure all ingredients are prepped and ready (mise en place).
  6. Add Peppers: Stir in the sliced green bell pepper and mild red chili, cooking for an additional 30-45 seconds.
  7. Combine Chips: Add the cooked chips to the wok, tossing to combine with the sautéed vegetables.
  8. Season the Chips: Sprinkle the prepared salt and pepper seasoning over the chips, ensuring even coverage.
  9. Finish with Spring Onions: Add the chopped spring onions and toss everything together until well combined.
  10. Serve Immediately: Enjoy your delicious salt and pepper chips right away!
