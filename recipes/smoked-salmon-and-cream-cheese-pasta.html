Smoked Salmon and Cream Cheese Pasta

Lunch & Dinner, Pasta & Grains
Aapprox 410 - 425 cal if 4 servings
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • 300 g (10.5 oz) pasta
  • 25 g (¼ stick) butter
  • 1 small red onion, finely chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 200 g (7 oz) smoked salmon, roughly chopped
  • 50 g white wine
  • 180 g (6.3 oz) light Philadelphia cream cheese
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • ½ bunch chives, chopped
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Method

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to boil. Cook pasta 1 minute less than package instructions. Reserve 2 cups of pasta water before draining.
  2. In a large pan, melt butter over medium heat. Sauté onion for 1 minute, then add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Add smoked salmon, cooking 2-3 minutes until light pink. Break larger pieces with a spatula.
  4. Pour in white wine, cooking for 1 minute until alcohol evaporates. Stir in cream cheese, letting it melt completely; add pasta water if needed. Mix in lemon juice.
  5. Combine pasta with the sauce, stirring until coated. Add chives, season with black pepper, and serve.
