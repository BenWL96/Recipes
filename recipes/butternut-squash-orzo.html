Butternut Squash & Orzo

Dinner, Pasta & Risotto
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • 1 small to medium-sized butternut squash, peeled, seeded, and diced (about 600-700g)
  • 1 small white onion, halved
  • 5 garlic cloves, peeled
  • 30 ml olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp sage
  • 150 ml vegetable stock
  • 100 ml double cream
  • 80 g grated Gruyère or Parmesan cheese
  • 100 g fresh spinach leaves
  • 300 g orzo pasta (or any type of pasta)
  • Optional toppings:
    • Fresh parsley, chopped
    • Grated Parmesan cheese
    • Toasted pine nuts

Method

  1. Preheat the Oven: Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F). On a baking sheet, combine the diced butternut squash, halved onions, and whole garlic cloves. Drizzle with 30 ml of olive oil and season with salt, black pepper, sage, and thyme. Roast for about 35-40 minutes or until the butternut squash is tender and slightly caramelized, and the onions and garlic are soft and browned.
  2. Blend the Vegetables: Once roasted, transfer the butternut squash, onions, and garlic to a food processor and blend until smooth.
  3. Prepare the Sauce: In a saucepan over medium heat, add the blended butternut squash mixture.
  4. Simmer: Stir in the vegetable stock and double cream. Allow the sauce to simmer gently for about 8-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened. Adjust seasoning with additional salt and black pepper to taste.
  5. Add Cheese and Spinach: Stir in the grated cheese and fresh spinach leaves, cooking until the spinach wilts and is incorporated into the sauce, about 2 minutes.
  6. Cook the Orzo: In a separate pot, cook the orzo pasta according to package instructions until al dente. Drain the orzo.
  7. Combine Pasta and Sauce: Toss the cooked orzo with the creamy butternut squash sauce until well combined.
  8. Serve: Serve hot, garnished with fresh parsley, additional grated Parmesan cheese, or toasted pine nuts, as desired.
