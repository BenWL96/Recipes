(Claire saffitz) Malted “Forever” Brownies

Desserts, Brownies

Recipe

  • Butter for the pan
  • ¼ cup Dutch-process cocoa powder (0.7 oz / 20g)
  • 5 ounces semisweet chocolate, 64-68% cacao, coarsely chopped (142g)
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, cut into pieces (3 oz / 85g)
  • ¼ cup neutral oil, such as vegetable or grapeseed (2 oz / 56g)
  • ½ cup granulated sugar (3.5 oz / 100g)
  • ½ cup packed dark brown sugar (3.5 oz / 100g)
  • 1 large egg (1.8 oz / 50g)
  • 2 large egg yolks (1.1 oz / 32g)
  • 1½ tsp vanilla extract
  • ¾ cup all-purpose flour (3.5 oz / 100g)
  • 2 tbsp malted milk powder (optional, 0.63 oz / 18g)
  • 1 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt (0.11 oz / 3g)
  • 6 ounces milk chocolate, coarsely chopped (170g)

Preparation:

  1. Preheat and Prepare Pan: Arrange an oven rack in the center position and preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Line an 8x8-inch pan with 2 sheets of foil, crossing them over one another and pressing into the corners. Lightly butter the foil and set aside.
  2. Bloom Cocoa: In a large heatproof bowl, whisk the cocoa powder with ¼ cup boiling water (4 oz / 113g) until smooth. This will enhance the flavor of the cocoa.
  3. Melt Chocolate, Butter, and Oil: Add the semisweet chocolate, butter, and oil to the bowl with the cocoa mixture. Set the bowl over a medium saucepan filled with about 1 inch of simmering water (ensuring the bowl doesn't touch the water). Stir occasionally until everything is melted and smooth. Remove the bowl from heat and let it cool to lukewarm.
  4. Add Sugars and Eggs: Whisk in the granulated sugar and brown sugar into the chocolate mixture (it may look grainy or separated, which is normal). Add the egg, egg yolks, and vanilla, whisking vigorously until the mixture is thick, smooth, and glossy.
  5. Mix Dry Ingredients: Add the flour, malted milk powder (if using), and salt to the chocolate mixture. Whisk slowly to combine, then whisk vigorously for 45 seconds until the batter becomes very thick.
  6. Fold in Chocolate and Bake: Fold the chopped milk chocolate into the batter with a spatula. Scrape the batter into the prepared pan, spreading it evenly to the edges.
  7. Bake: Bake the brownies until the surface is shiny and puffed, and the center is dry to the touch but still soft when pressed, 25-30 minutes.
  8. Cool, Chill, and Cut: Let the brownies cool in the pan for about 1 hour. Refrigerate for another hour until the pan feels cold for a chewier texture. Use the foil to lift the brownies out, transfer to a cutting board, and slice into 16 squares.
