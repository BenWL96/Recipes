Braised chicken legs (kikkoman)

Lunch & Dinner, Curries & Thai
Approx 650 caloriesOriginal recipe · kikkomanusa.com ↗
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken legs
  • 1 small onion, slivered
  • 2 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
  • 2 teaspoons ginger, minced
  • 1 - 2 jalapenos (or ½ fresno chilli)
  • 1 teaspoon tomato paste
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 teaspoons brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • Bunch of fresh cilantro

Method

  1. Start by preheating your oven to 175c. Pat the chicken legs dry and season both sides generously with salt and pepper. ​
  2. Heat a generous amount of neutral oil over medium-high heat in a pan and add in the chicken legs skin side down. Sear skin
  3. side down for 5 minutes until brown, then flip over and sear for another 3 minutes. Remove then set aside.​
  4. In the same pan over medium heat, add onions, garlic, ginger, and chilis and sauté until onions are just translucent (about 1 minute).​
  5. Add in the tomato paste and mix. Continue cooking until slightly brown (about another 1-2 minutes).​
  6. Turn heat up to high, then pour in chicken stock to deglaze, then the coconut milk, soy sauce, lime juice, and brown sugar. Then add in the peppercorns.​
  7. Bring to a boil, then lay in chicken legs skin side up and place in the oven to braise, uncovered, for 45 minutes.​
  8. Remove from the oven, remove chicken legs and set aside, and bring the remaining braising liquid to a boil and reduce by about half.​
  9. Plate your chicken leg, spoon over the sauce, and garnish with a handful of fresh cilantro leaves.
