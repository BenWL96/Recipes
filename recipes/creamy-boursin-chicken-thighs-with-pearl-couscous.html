Creamy Boursin Chicken Thighs with Pearl Couscous and Kale

Lunch & Dinner, Pasta & Grains
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • Approx 585 - 610 cal per serving (4 servings - using 4 thighs)

(link)

  • 2-4 chicken thighs (skin-on, bone-in preferred)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 8 oz pearl couscous (approximately 227 g)
  • 1 cup dry white wine
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • ½ package Boursin cheese (garlic & herb)
  • Big handful of kale, chopped (remove stems)

Method

  1. Prep the Chicken: Take the chicken thighs out of the refrigerator about 20-30 minutes prior to cooking for more even cooking. Pat dry to ensure crispy skin and season generously with salt on both sides.
  2. Sear the Chicken: Heat a skillet over medium heat. Add olive oil and butter, letting it melt. Sear the chicken thighs skin-side down for about 8-10 minutes until golden brown, avoiding movement to ensure crispiness. Flip the chicken and sear for another 15-20 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C). Remove and set aside.
  3. Cook Shallots: With the chicken removed, reduce the heat slightly. In the same pan, add the minced shallot and stir for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Toast Couscous: Add the pearl couscous to the pan and stir to combine. Let it toast for about a minute until it becomes nutty.
  5. Deglaze: Pour in the white wine and stir until fully absorbed. Then add the chicken stock and stir.
  6. Combine Chicken and Couscous: Increase the heat back to medium until it begins to bubble, then reduce to low-medium. Return the chicken thighs to the pearl couscous and broth mixture.
  7. Finish the Dish: Once the pearl couscous has absorbed all the liquid, remove the chicken thighs again. Stir in the Boursin cheese and chopped kale until combined and creamy.
  8. Serve: Taste and adjust seasoning as desired. Serve warm, optionally garnished with fresh chives or Italian parsley.
