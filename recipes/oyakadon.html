Oyakadon

Lunch & Dinner, Japanese
3 servings
IngredientsMethod

Ingredients

  • 1 onion (¼ 1 serving) - thin slices
  • 3 green onions - cut diagonally
  • Roughly 3 chicken thighs
  • 6 eggs

Seasoning ingredients:

  1. 1 cup water
  2. 1.5 tsp dashi powder
  3. 3 tbsp soy sauce
  4. 3 tbsp mirin
  5. 3 tsp sugar

Method

  1. Cut chicken into bite size pieces, cut chicken along the grain into strips 2.5 cm wide then angle your knife back diagonally and slice chicke strips against the grain into flat pieces.
  2. Crack eggs into small bowl and lightly beat the eggs.
  3. Without turning on heat, add 1 cup water, 1.5 dashi powder, 3 tbsp soy sauce and 3 tbsp mirin to pot
  4. Add 3 tsp sugar, onion and white part of green onion
  5. Place chicken pieces onto top of the onion in single layer. Turn hear to medium high.
  6. Cover with lid and cook for 3 mins
  7. Flip chicken to cook other side. If broth is evaporating too quickly add 1 - 2 tbsp water.
  8. Cook for additional 1 - 2 mins then reduce head to medium and open lid.
  9. Drizzle beaten egg in a spiral pattern over chicken and onions
  10. Sprinkle green part of onion ontop. Cook until egg just sets.
